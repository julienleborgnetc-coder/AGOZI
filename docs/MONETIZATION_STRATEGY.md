# AGOZI — Monetization Strategy

Status: planned for later activation, target around 10,000 downloads.

## Core principle

AGOZI personal/local use should remain easy to adopt and largely free.
Monetization should focus on collaboration/sharing rather than artificially restricting basic personal use.

## Proposed funnel

### Free local
- No account required
- Up to 10 tasks
- Local/private use
- Core reminders and categories remain free

### Free account
- Required after 10 tasks
- Unlimited personal tasks
- Personal cloud backup/sync when authenticated
- Still free if the user does not use sharing

### AGOZI Share
- €1.99/month
- Proposed annual option: €19.99/year
- One paying user can participate in shared AGOZI items

### AGOZI Duo
- €2.99/month
- Proposed annual option: €29.99/year
- One subscription covers the payer + one other person
- Intended as the primary commercial offer

Potential later tier only if demand exists:
- Family: around €3.99–€4.99/month for a larger group

## Product rationale

The paywall should appear on a high-intent action: sharing with another person.
Do not paywall basic reminders, categories, dark mode, history, or other fundamental personal-use features.

Desired positioning:

> AGOZI personal = free.
> AGOZI together = paid.

The Duo offer should reduce friction where one partner is willing to pay but the invited person does not want a separate subscription.

## Revenue assumptions

Do not model 20–30% of all downloads as paying users.
20–30% account creation may be plausible if registration is requested only after meaningful use.

At 10,000 downloads, use a working planning range of roughly 100–300 paying subscriptions initially, then measure actual conversion.

Primary monetization KPI:
- percentage of users who reach the Share action and then subscribe

Secondary KPIs:
- % of users creating an account
- % reaching 10 tasks
- % attempting sharing
- share-paywall conversion
- monthly/annual mix
- Duo vs Solo mix
- churn/retention of paying users

## Launch timing

Do not implement monetization immediately.
First observe real user behavior:
- account creation rate
- number of users exceeding 10 tasks
- use of sharing
- retention

Use those metrics to validate or adjust the paywall before activating subscriptions.

## Current preferred offer

- Free local: 10 tasks
- Free account: unlimited personal tasks + cloud backup/sync
- Share: €1.99/month or €19.99/year
- Duo: €2.99/month or €29.99/year

No lifetime purchase initially because shared/cloud users create recurring infrastructure costs.
