# AGOZI — Growth Strategy 0 → 100K Downloads

Status: strategic plan
Scope: worldwide Android launch, organic + paid acquisition
Primary objective: reach 100,000 quality downloads without sacrificing retention or unit economics.

## 1. North-star principle

Do not optimize for raw installs. Optimize for installs that become useful users.

The acquisition funnel should be measured as:
Store impression / external view → Store click → Install → First task created → 3 tasks created → Day-7 retained → Account created → Sharing attempted → Paid conversion later.

Until monetization launches, the most important business-quality metrics are:
- install to first task
- install to 3 tasks
- D1 / D7 / D30 retention where available
- % reaching 10 tasks
- % creating an account
- % attempting sharing
- invitations sent / accepted
- uninstall / crash / ANR signals
- Play Store conversion by country, channel and listing

## 2. Positioning

AGOZI should NOT be marketed as a generic todo-list or habit tracker.

Core problem statement:
"When did I last...?"

Core product promise:
Remember recurring life tasks without turning them into a stressful todo list.

Message pillars:
1. Home: sheets, filters, cleaning, maintenance
2. Pets: flea treatment, grooming, vet-related recurring care
3. Plants: watering, repotting, fertilizing
4. Car: service, tires, fluids, maintenance
5. Personal/admin: documents, renewals, recurring life chores
6. Couples: shared responsibility without asking "who did it last?"

## 3. Market evidence

Large adjacent apps prove demand:
- Tody: 1M+ Google Play downloads
- Nipto: 500K+ Google Play downloads
- Tidy: 100K+ Google Play downloads

Very close "when did I last" apps remain much smaller, meaning AGOZI can compete by owning the broad category rather than household cleaning only.

Competitive edge to emphasize:
- ultra-simple
- local/private without account
- no streak pressure
- not a todo list
- categories spanning all of life
- optional account
- real shared tasks
- multilingual/global

## 4. Growth model

Target growth mix at scale:
- 35–50% Google Play organic/search/explore
- 20–30% short-form social organic
- 10–20% sharing/referral/invitations
- 10–25% paid acquisition

The exact mix must be driven by measured cohorts, not fixed in advance.

## 5. Phase 0: instrumentation before scale

AGOZI intentionally avoids invasive analytics. Keep that philosophy.

Use the lowest-intrusion measurement stack possible:
- Google Play Console acquisition reports
- UTM-tagged Play links by channel/campaign
- Google Ads native app conversion signals when campaigns start
- campaign-specific custom store listing URLs
- aggregate server-side counts only where already naturally available for authenticated features

Do not add Meta/TikTok SDKs merely to chase install volume before there is evidence those channels work organically.

Create a weekly growth sheet with:
country, channel, store visitors/clicks, installs, conversion rate, rating, reviews, account creations, sharing attempts, accepted invitations, paid spend, CPI, retained-user proxy.

## 7. Screenshot architecture

Screenshot 1 must explain the app without reading the description.

Suggested sequence:
1. "When did you last do it?"
2. "See what is overdue at a glance"
3. "One tap when it's done"
4. "Get reminded only when it matters"
5. "Everything stays private on your phone"
6. "Share selected tasks with someone"
7. market-specific use cases

Every major market should test at least one culturally relevant set of examples.

## 9. Organic short-form content machine

Main channels:
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts

Do not create polished corporate ads first.
Create native "problem → reveal → app" content.

### Content formats

A. Question hook
"When did you last change your bedsheets?"
"Do you actually remember when you changed your water filter?"
"When did your dog last get flea treatment?"

B. Memory failure
POV: you realize you have no idea whether it was 2 weeks or 2 months ago.

C. Couple friction
"Did you water the plants?"
"I thought you did."

D. Tiny demo
6–12 seconds:
open AGOZI → see overdue → Done → timer resets.

E. Lists
"5 things everyone forgets to track"
"7 home tasks that aren't daily todos"

F. Emotional relief
"Stop keeping recurring chores in your head."

### Creative rule
One video = one use case.
Do not explain the whole app.

### Production target
First 90 days:
- 5–7 unique short-form concepts per week
- each concept adapted into 2–4 hooks
- repost natively on all three platforms
- translate only winners

A winning organic creative becomes:
1. more organic variants
2. paid creative
3. store listing screenshot/headline
4. SEO page angle

## 10. Creator seeding

Do not start with celebrities.

Target nano/micro creators whose audience matches actual use cases:
- cleaning/home organization
- pet owners
- plant creators
- car maintenance / DIY
- ADHD / mental-load / organization creators, without making medical claims
- couples / home-life creators
- minimalist productivity

Preferred deal at first:
- free app access where relevant
- simple demo request
- small fixed fee only after creative quality is visible
- permission to run winning content as Spark/whitelisted ads where platform rules permit

Track every creator with a unique UTM/custom store listing URL.

## 11. Community distribution

Use communities for relevance, not spam.

Targets:
- Reddit communities around productivity, home maintenance, plants, pets, Android apps, indie apps
- Facebook groups by use case
- Product Hunt / indie launch communities when public launch is ready
- Android-focused forums/directories
- newsletters that curate useful apps

Post:
- the problem and what was built
- a short GIF/video
- invite feedback
- avoid mass copy-paste promotion

Goal at 0–1K is insight and advocates, not volume.

## 12. Referral / sharing loop

AGOZI already contains the beginning of a viral loop.

Target loop:
A installs → A uses → A shares a real task with B → B installs → B receives immediate value.

Improve later without dark patterns:
- clean invite deep link
- invitation explains the exact shared task
- recipient lands directly in the relevant flow after install/sign-in
- optionally show "Shared with you on AGOZI" outside the app via safe invitation copy
- never require the recipient to pay merely to accept during the growth phase before monetization

Track:
invites per active sharer
invite acceptance rate
new installs from invite links
share-to-second-user coefficient

## 13. Ratings and reviews

Never ask immediately after install.

Trigger the Play review prompt after a genuine success moment, e.g.:
- several successful Done actions
- user has returned multiple times
- no recent error state

Do not gate or manipulate ratings.

Negative feedback should go to Feedback/support; rating prompt timing should remain neutral and policy compliant.

## 14. Paid acquisition philosophy

Paid media is a laboratory first, accelerator second.

Do NOT buy large worldwide install volume before knowing:
- which use cases retain
- which countries convert
- which creatives work
- which Play listing converts

### Stage A: €150–€300 learning budget
Only after public availability.

Channels:
1. Google App Campaigns
2. TikTok only if organic videos show promise
3. Meta only after there are proven short-form creatives

Google first is logical because intent + Play ecosystem + native app-install optimization reduce operational complexity.

### Stage B: €500–€1,500 validation
Scale only combinations that meet quality thresholds.

Separate country clusters when economics differ:
- Tier 1: US/UK/CA/AU/DE/FR
- Southern Europe: ES/IT/PT
- Growth markets: BR/MX/TR/PL/ID
- test Asia separately: JP/KR

Never mix countries with radically different CPI and retention in one learning bucket if it prevents diagnosis.

### Stage C: scaling
Increase budget gradually, generally avoiding sudden >20% jumps unless the platform specifically recommends otherwise.
Scale winning creative-country-listing combinations, not the whole world at once.

## 15. Google App Campaigns

Initial objective: install volume only while event volume is too low.

Once a meaningful in-app action reaches sufficient daily volume, optimize toward that event rather than raw installs.

Candidate future optimization events:
- create_first_task
- create_third_task
- day_7_active
- create_account
- share_attempt

Google recommends sufficient conversion volume and materially larger budgets when optimizing toward in-app actions. Do not prematurely optimize to an event with sparse data.

Creative library:
- multiple standalone text assets
- portrait, square and landscape images
- 10–30 second video variants
- use all practical asset slots
- replace weak assets regularly

## 16. TikTok paid

Only promote concepts already showing organic signal where possible.

Creative:
- hook in first 1–2 seconds
- native phone-camera feel
- one use case
- visible AGOZI interaction
- captions on-screen
- 8–20 seconds preferred for first tests

Test 2–3 variants at a time.
Change one meaningful variable:
hook, use case, speaker, opening frame, CTA.

Spark Ads are preferred when a creator or organic post already has credible engagement.

## 17. Meta paid

Use after creative-market fit is emerging.

Creative buckets:
- UGC problem/solution
- simple screen demo
- couple/shared-task scenario
- carousel of forgotten recurring tasks

Do not judge campaigns on CPI alone.
Compare:
- CPI
- cost per account
- cost per share attempt
- retained-user proxy
- later, CAC vs subscription LTV

## 18. Budget guardrails

Early:
- no broad campaign should spend more than a predefined learning cap without generating useful downstream behavior
- pause creative quickly when it produces cheap installs but poor activation
- do not scale a country because CPI is cheap if users vanish immediately

Suggested initial paid cadence:
Week 1: €5–10/day total
Week 2: €10–20/day only if quality is acceptable
Later: scale winners in controlled increments

The purpose of the first €300 is learning, not reaching 100K.

## 19. Country strategy

Do not launch marketing equally in 100+ countries.

Wave 1 — language/market validation:
US, UK, France, Germany, Spain, Brazil

Wave 2 — lower-cost expansion if retention supports it:
Mexico, Italy, Poland, Türkiye, Indonesia

Wave 3 — premium-localization markets:
Japan, South Korea

Use actual Play Console data to reorder this list.

## 20. 0 → 100K operating phases

### 0 → 1K
Goal: find who cares and what message works.
Mostly organic.
Success criterion: repeatable content/store angle and real retention.

### 1K → 10K
Goal: find one repeatable acquisition channel.
Add small paid tests.
Build local store listings.
Collect reviews.
Identify 2–3 best use cases and countries.

### 10K → 30K
Goal: scale proven combinations.
Introduce monetization tests separately from acquisition experiments.
Seed 20–50 nano/micro creators.
Turn winning organic videos into paid.

### 30K → 100K
Goal: industrialize.
Localized content factory.
Paid scale on proven cohorts.
SEO pages compounded over time.
Referral/share loop.
Creator licensing/whitelisting.
Google Play experiments continuously.

## 24. Things NOT to do

- buy incentivized installs
- buy reviews
- spam Reddit/Facebook groups
- scale only because CPI is low
- translate one generic ad into 29 languages before identifying a winning concept
- position AGOZI as "another task manager"
- add invasive analytics simply because ad platforms request more signals
- run many countries and audiences in one opaque campaign too early
- keep weak creatives alive for weeks
- launch monetization and major acquisition changes simultaneously if it makes learning impossible

## 25. Current strategic priority

Until the public launch produces meaningful traffic:

1. Play Store conversion
2. short-form organic content
3. website SEO/use-case pages
4. invite/share loop quality
5. reviews
6. small creator outreach
7. paid acquisition only after the above generates initial signal

The goal is not 100K cheap installs.
The goal is 100K people who understand why AGOZI exists.
