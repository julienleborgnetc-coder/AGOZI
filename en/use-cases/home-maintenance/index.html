Use case

Home maintenance reminders with AGOZI

Keep the last maintenance date and next due date without managing a complicated household calendar.

Why household tasks are easy to forget

Some actions are neither daily nor important enough to become an appointment. You remember that a filter, appliance or particular textile needs attention, but not the exact date it was last handled.

AGOZI covers that middle ground: record the last completion, choose your own interval and immediately see what is overdue, due soon or still up to date.

Examples of home maintenance tracking

Filters and ventilation

Keep the date of a replacement or cleaning and use the interval recommended for your own equipment.

Household appliances

Record descaling, filter cleaning or any periodic maintenance described by the manufacturer.

Linen and textiles

Remember bedsheets, duvets, curtains or other textiles whose schedule does not fit a daily to-do list.

Occasional cleaning

Add areas or objects you clean from time to time and simply want to remember.

A schedule that fits your home

AGOZI does not impose a universal frequency. The appropriate interval depends on the equipment, manufacturer, usage and your situation. Set it in days, weeks or calendar months, then change it if your routine changes.

Share only what concerns two people

If maintenance is handled by two people, that item can be shared after creating an account. Participants see the same state and shared history for that item. Your other private tracking is not automatically uploaded.

What AGOZI replaces — and what it does not

AGOZI saves you from remembering a date or searching through a calendar. It does not replace an appliance manual, professional diagnosis or manufacturer safety guidance. You choose the schedule; the app helps you follow it.

Availability: AGOZI is currently in closed testing on Android and is not yet publicly available on Google Play.
