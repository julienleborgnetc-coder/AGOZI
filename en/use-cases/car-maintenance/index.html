Use case

Track car maintenance with AGOZI

Record the last maintenance date and let AGOZI remind you about its next time-based due date.

Find the last date without searching

For an occasional action such as cleaning, an inspection or a maintenance operation, you may remember what was done but not the exact month. AGOZI keeps the last completion and calculates the next date from the interval you choose.

Examples of car-related tracking

Periodic maintenance

Record an operation scheduled by time and use the guidance that applies to your own vehicle.

Cleaning and protection

Keep track of a deep clean, interior care or a protective treatment performed occasionally.

Personal checks

Create checks you want to remember without treating the app as a professional inspection.

Seasonal equipment

Track a recurring action related to your usage or a season with an interval you choose.

What the app tracks

AGOZI tracks a time-based due date: days, weeks or calendar months after the last completion. It does not read your odometer, connect to the vehicle or automatically combine a “date or mileage” requirement. Always follow the manufacturer and qualified professional advice for vehicle maintenance.

After maintenance

Tap “Done today” to record the new date. The history keeps the completion and the next cycle starts automatically. If the completion was accidental, you can undo it immediately.

A shared item when two people use the car

You can share only the relevant item. Its state and shared history remain synchronized between participants, while your other private items stay local.

Availability: AGOZI is currently in closed testing on Android and is not yet publicly available on Google Play.
