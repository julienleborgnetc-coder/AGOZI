Frequently asked questions

AGOZI in a few answers.

Is AGOZI a to-do list?

AGOZI is not a general to-do list. It remembers the last completion of a recurring action, calculates its next due date and reminds you when it is time to do it again.

Do I need an account?

No. Private tracking works without an account. An account is only useful when you choose to share an item with someone else.

What kinds of due dates can I track?

Home or car maintenance, pet and plant care, administrative checks, periodic cleaning, or any custom action you choose.

Does AGOZI replace a calendar?

No. A calendar is ideal for appointments fixed to a specific date. AGOZI is designed for actions whose next due date depends on when they were last completed.
