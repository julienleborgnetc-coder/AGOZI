04 — A few useful details

A few answers.
That’s all.

Something else on your mind?
We’re here to help

Is AGOZI available yet?

AGOZI is currently in closed testing on Android. It is not yet publicly available on Google Play.

Do I need to pay or create an account?

AGOZI is free and ad-free. Private tracking requires no account. An account is only needed if you choose to share an item with another person.

Is it a to-do list or a calendar?

Neither a general to-do list nor an appointment calendar. AGOZI remembers when you last completed a recurring task and calculates its next due date using your chosen interval.

How do reminders work?

Notifications are scheduled locally, on the due date and again 3 and 7 days later if needed. Their time is configurable. Android settings and notification permissions may affect delivery.

What if I mark a task as done by mistake?

An undo option lets you reverse an accidental completion. The history helps you keep track of what was done.
